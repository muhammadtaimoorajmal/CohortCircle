# Semi-Structured Interview Note-Taking Template: CohortCircle

> **Usage:** Standardized recording sheet for transcribing and coding one-on-one student interviews (`INT-01` through `INT-07`).  
> **Privacy Governance:** Completed records containing personal identifying details remain in `private-research-data/`. Only de-identified excerpts linked to Participant IDs (`P-01` to `P-07`) may be incorporated into public evidence tables.

# Research Ethics, Participant Rights & Privacy Protocol

> **Applicability:** All empirical activities conducted across the CohortCircle project lifecycle, including Module 02 (Contextual Inquiry, Interviews, Surveys) and Module 09 (Summative Usability Testing).

---

## 1. Ethical Governance Principles

CohortCircle adheres strictly to established ethical codes for human-centered computing research, including the ACM Code of Ethics and the Declaration of Helsinki principles for human research:

1. **Informed & Explicit Consent:** Every participant must receive full, plain-language disclosure regarding the study objectives, procedures, and data handling before participating.
2. **Voluntary Participation & Right to Withdraw:** Participation is completely voluntary. Any participant may decline to answer any question, pause the session, or withdraw at any point without penalty or need for justification.
3. **Data Minimization:** Only data strictly necessary for evaluating usability and understanding peer learning needs will be collected. We strictly avoid asking for contact details, home addresses, official student IDs, or academic transcripts.
4. **Anonymization & Pseudonymization:** Raw research logs, interview transcripts, and survey data will be de-identified immediately upon collection, replacing real names with coded identifiers (e.g., `P-01`, `P-02`).
5. **No Commercial or Third-Party Data Transfer:** Collected data will never be sold, licensed, or shared with third-party advertisers or commercial brokers.

---

## 2. Participant Privacy & Recording Protocols

### 2.1 Audio & Screen Recording Opt-In
* Recording (audio or screen interaction) is **never mandatory**.
* Observers/interviewers must request separate, explicit permission before initiating any recording.
* If a participant declines recording, the session proceeds using manual handwritten/typed notes only.
* No video of participants' faces or surroundings will be recorded; screen capture is restricted strictly to the application interface.

### 2.2 Photography & Contextual Inquiry Rules
* In campus public spaces (libraries, common areas), observations will focus strictly on spatial workflows, tool interactions, and behavioral patterns.
* No identifiable faces or personal screen contents (e.g., personal emails or private chats) will be photographed or transcribed.

### 2.3 Data Retention & Secure Disposal
* Raw audio recordings will be transcribed into anonymized markdown notes within 5 business days of recording.
* Once transcription is verified, raw audio files will be permanently erased.
* Anonymized research notes will be archived in the project research repository with access restricted to the core research team.

Your participation in this study is entirely voluntary. You may choose not to
participate, skip any questions, take breaks, or stop your participation at any
time without any negative consequence.

Depending on the session, you will be invited to participate in:

open-source documentation, or presentation. A coded pseudonym (e.g., P-01) will
be used to represent your feedback.

PARTICIPANT CONFIRMATION:

understood this information and voluntarily agree to participate.

Participant Name: __________________________________________________
Participant Signature / Confirmation: _____________________________

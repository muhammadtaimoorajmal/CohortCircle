# Persona Validation & Empirical Elevation Plan: CohortCircle

> **Methodology:** Post-Fieldwork Hypothesis Testing & Behavioral Model Calibration  
> **Status:** **OPERATIONAL PROTOCOL (PREPARED FOR POST-FIELDWORK EXECUTION)**  
> **Purpose:** Defines the exact criteria, data-mapping steps, and falsification rules required to graduate unvalidated proto-personas (`PROTO-LEARN-01`, `PROTO-LEARN-02`, `PROTO-HOST-01`) into verified empirical personas.

---

## 1. Operational Elevation Pipeline

Upon the completion and transcription of Module 02 fieldwork, the validation pipeline executes in four structured phases:

```
[Phase 1: Behavioral Variable Extraction]
  └── Extract participant spectrum ratings from INT-01..07 and survey distributions
            │
[Phase 2: Clustering & Mapping]
  └── Map real participants against the 6 Behavioral Spectrum Axes (PERSONA_DEVELOPMENT.md)
            │
[Phase 3: Falsification & Fit Assessment]
  └── Evaluate whether empirical clusters confirm, split, or invalidate proto-personas
            │
[Phase 4: Formal Persona Elevation]
  └── Update PERSONA_CATALOG.md: Replace PROTO- prefixes with VERIFIED empirical personas
```

---

## 2. Behavioral Spectrum Mapping Rubric

Each participant transcript (`INT-01` to `INT-07`) will be scored along the six behavioral axes:

| Behavioral Axis | Low Endpoint (Score = 1) | Midpoint (Score = 3) | High Endpoint (Score = 5) | Qualifying Interview Probes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Axis 1: Study Modality** | Exclusively Silent Co-Study | Mixed (Silent with verbal breaks) | Exclusively Vocal Discussion | `Q02`, `Q07`, `OBS-01` |
| **Axis 2: Schedule Flexibility** | Highly Constrained / Rigid Commuter | Moderate (Evenings/Weekends only) | Highly Flexible Residential | `Q06`, `SUR-Q05` |
| **Axis 3: Initiative & Role** | Completely Passive / Joiner | Co-Collaborator | Proactive Host / Organizer | `Q03`, `Q09`, `INT-05` |
| **Axis 4: Pacing & Horizon** | Deadline Sprint (Exams only) | Weekly Assignment Cadence | Long-Term Conceptual Mastery | `Q01`, `SUR-Q02` |
| **Axis 5: Group Size Cap** | Intimate Pair / Triad (2–3) | Small Circle (4–5) | Large Cohort (6–8+) | `Q02`, `Q09`, `SUR-Q08` |
| **Axis 6: Multi-Tool Tolerance** | Minimalist (Single app only) | Pragmatic (Uses what group uses) | Power User (Discord/Notion/Bot) | `Q08`, `OBS-02` |

## 3. Explicit Falsification & Retirement Criteria

A proto-persona is **falsified or retired** if any of the following empirical conditions occur:

1. **Zero Participant Representation:** If none of the 5–7 interviewed student participants (`INT-01` to `INT-07`) exhibit the behavioral cluster associated with a proto-persona, that persona is declared invalid and retired.
2. **Survey Disconfirmation (< 15% Preference):** If exploratory survey analysis reveals that a proto-persona's core preferred study format represents less than 15% of respondents, it cannot be sustained as a primary persona.
3. **Cluster Fusion:** If interview evidence reveals that the distinction between `PROTO-LEARN-01` (Focused Solver) and `PROTO-LEARN-02` (Discussion Seeker) does not exist in practice (e.g., all learners switch dynamically between both styles within the same session), they will be merged into a single multi-modal learner persona.
4. **Host Role Disconfirmation:** If no interviewed participants express a willingness to host or facilitate circles, `PROTO-HOST-01` will be re-modeled as an automated or decentralized system facilitation mechanism rather than a distinct user archetype.

## 4. Persona Elevation Verification Checklist

Prior to removing the `UNVALIDATED PROTO-PERSONA` label, the research lead must verify:
- [ ] At least 2 interview transcripts (`INT-XX`) cited with authentic de-identified excerpts supporting the persona's goals and pain points.
- [ ] At least 1 contextual inquiry observation (`OBS-XX`) corroborating the persona's physical/digital behaviors.
- [ ] Survey frequency distributions (`SUR-DATA`) cited confirming statistical relevance.
- [ ] All 13 standardized persona dimensions updated with empirical findings.
- [ ] Falsification criteria reviewed and cleared.
- [ ] Module 03 Handoff report updated and committed.

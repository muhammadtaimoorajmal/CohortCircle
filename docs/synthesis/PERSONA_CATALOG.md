## Persona 01: The Independent Focused Problem-Solver (`PROTO-LEARN-01`)

* **Role:** Primary User — Learner
* **Archetype Identifier:** `PROTO-LEARN-01`
* **Status:** `UNVALIDATED PROTO-PERSONA (HYPOTHESIS BASELINE)`

### 1. Profile Summary & Description
An upper-division university student or technical self-directed learner who values quiet focus, rigorous problem set completion, and mutual accountability without unnecessary conversational overhead.

### 2. Relevant Learning Context
* Enrolled in mathematically or technically demanding subjects (e.g., Algorithms, Discrete Math, Systems Programming).
* Spends substantial time debugging, solving practice problem sets, and writing assignments under tight weekly deadlines.
* Often commutes to campus; has rigid non-study commitments (part-time technical work or fixed transit times).

### 3. Goals & Motivations
* **Primary Goal:** Complete challenging problem sets faster and with fewer conceptual blocks through focused peer accountability.
* **Secondary Goal:** Find 1 to 3 reliable peers who share the same syllabus pacing and working hours.
* **Motivation:** Knows that studying alongside someone prevents procrastination, but dislikes groups that dissolve into social distraction.

### 4. Behaviors & Habits
* Studies primarily in library silent zones or with noise-canceling headphones.
* Uses split-screen workstations (IDE/problem set on one side, reference docs on the other).
* Dislikes unstructured group chats; mutes class WhatsApp/Discord channels due to notification fatigue.

### 5. Study Preferences
* **Primary Preferred Styles:** *Silent Co-Study* (60%) and *Collaborative Problem-Solving* (40%).
* **Preferred Group Size:** Intimate micro-circle (2 to 4 peers maximum).
* **Preferred Cadence:** 90-minute blocks, 1 to 2 times weekly, strictly adhering to start and end times.
* **Preferred Modality:** Hybrid (In-person on campus during class days; online evening sessions during transit/work days).

### 6. Scheduling Constraints
* Commute travel times (>45 mins) create rigid, non-negotiable arrival and departure times on campus.
* Available primarily during specific weekday evening blocks (18:00–21:00) and Saturday mornings.

### 7. Frustrations & Unmet Needs
* **Flakiness & Late Cancellations:** Peers who confirm attendance and then cancel 15 minutes before the session.
* **Mismatched Work Ethic:** Showing up to collaborate only to find group members asking to copy solutions without working through the problems.
* **Off-Topic Banter:** Frustrated when a scheduled 2-hour study session contains only 30 minutes of real work and 90 minutes of casual chatter.

### 8. Trust, Privacy & Safety Expectations
* Wants to view explicit circle expectations (study style, rules on homework copying, agenda) *before* requesting to join.
* Prefers pseudonymous participation until mutual trust is established.

### 9. Accessibility Considerations
* Requires full keyboard navigability for rapid desktop management during coding workflow.
* Sensitive to excessive visual clutter, animations, and non-essential notifications.

### 10. Key Product Opportunities
* Transparent compatibility scoring highlighting schedule overlap and exact study style match (`FR-009`, `FR-010`).
* Silent Co-Study and Problem-Solving filter badges (`FR-007`).
* Structured session creation with explicit agendas and capacity caps (`FR-016`, `FR-020`).

### 11. Traceability & Supporting Evidence References
* **Mapped Research Inquiries:** `RQ-01`, `RQ-03`, `RQ-04`, `RQ-07`
* **Hypothesis Pointer:** `HYP-01`, `HYP-EM-L01`, `HYP-EM-L02`
* **Post-Fieldwork Validation Sources:** `INT-01`, `INT-02`, `OBS-01`, `SUR-Q05`

### 12. Confidence & Limitations
* **Confidence Level:** Moderate (strongly supported by common domain patterns; awaiting empirical student interview verification).
* **Limitations:** Assumes high intrinsic motivation; needs validation regarding willingness to use an external web tool over local Discord.

## Persona 02: The Conceptual Discussion Seeker (`PROTO-LEARN-02`)

* **Role:** Primary User — Learner
* **Archetype Identifier:** `PROTO-LEARN-02`
* **Status:** `UNVALIDATED PROTO-PERSONA (HYPOTHESIS BASELINE)`

### 1. Profile Summary & Description
A student in conceptual, qualitative, or interdisciplinary fields who learns most effectively by vocalizing ideas, debating arguments, questioning lecture assumptions, and collaboratively reviewing reading materials.

### 2. Relevant Learning Context
* Enrolled in courses with heavy reading loads, essay requirements, or qualitative conceptual mastery (e.g., Social Sciences, Humanities, Management, Cognitive Science).
* Attends large lecture classes (150–400 students) where direct interaction with teaching staff is limited.
* Lives on campus or within walking distance; has moderate scheduling flexibility between afternoon lectures.

### 3. Goals & Motivations
* **Primary Goal:** Clarify complex lecture concepts and readings through discussion, debate, and peer explanation.
* **Secondary Goal:** Overcome feelings of academic isolation and build a supportive peer learning network.
* **Motivation:** Understands concepts far deeper when forced to articulate and defend them aloud to a peer.

### 4. Behaviors & Habits
* Frequently visits campus lounges, department commons, and study cafes.
* Takes handwritten or tablet notes; highlights physical readings; creates summary outlines.
* Hesitates to reach out to strangers in large lecture halls due to social anxiety or fear of seeming needy.

### 5. Study Preferences
* **Primary Preferred Styles:** *Discussion-Based Learning* (50%), *Peer Teaching* (30%), and *Examination Preparation* (20%).
* **Preferred Group Size:** Small cohort (3 to 6 peers).
* **Preferred Cadence:** 60 to 90 minutes weekly, following major weekly lecture milestones.
* **Preferred Modality:** In-person study rooms or lounges; online video calls when weather or campus access is restricted.

### 6. Scheduling Constraints
* Schedule is fragmented by sporadic lecture hours throughout the day (e.g., 10:00–11:30 and 14:00–15:30).
* Seeks study circles that can meet in open campus gaps between classes.

### 7. Frustrations & Unmet Needs
* **Social Discovery Barrier:** Extremely difficult to know which of the 300 silent faces in a lecture hall are seeking a study partner.
* **Dominant Voices:** Frustrated when a study group is hijacked by a single aggressive peer who lectures rather than discusses.
* **Lack of Meeting Cadence:** Groups meet once before Midterm 1, feel great, and then never schedule Midterm 2 prep because no one took ownership.

### 8. Trust, Privacy & Safety Expectations
* Requires psychological safety: Needs to know that the circle is an open, non-judgmental learning space.
* Values request-to-join workflows with introductory notes to ensure mutual conversational compatibility.

### 9. Accessibility Considerations
* Requires high-contrast text and readable typography across tablet and mobile viewports (`NFR-A11Y-01`, `NFR-RESP-01`).
* Screen reader semantics for form inputs and status updates (`NFR-A11Y-04`).

### 10. Key Product Opportunities
* Subject keyword search and syllabus topic matching (`FR-006`).
* Request-to-join introductory notes allowing learners to explain their conversational goals (`FR-014`).
* Clear display of circle discussion norms and learning goals (`FR-012`).

### 11. Traceability & Supporting Evidence References
* **Mapped Research Inquiries:** `RQ-01`, `RQ-02`, `RQ-05`, `RQ-06`, `RQ-08`
* **Hypothesis Pointer:** `HYP-01`, `HYP-EM-L03`, `HYP-EM-L07`
* **Post-Fieldwork Validation Sources:** `INT-03`, `INT-06`, `OBS-02`, `SUR-Q04`

### 12. Confidence & Limitations
* **Confidence Level:** Moderate (reflects known lecture-hall isolation dynamics; needs empirical validation on preferred group size).
* **Limitations:** Needs verification on whether discussion seekers prefer in-person campus spots over remote Discord voice channels.

## Persona 03: The Collaborative Cohort Facilitator (`PROTO-HOST-01`)

* **Role:** Secondary User — Circle Host / Facilitator
* **Archetype Identifier:** `PROTO-HOST-01`
* **Status:** `UNVALIDATED PROTO-PERSONA (HYPOTHESIS BASELINE)`

### 1. Profile Summary & Description
A proactive student leader, peer tutor, or organized senior student who takes the initiative to convene, structure, and facilitate a recurring study circle to achieve shared academic milestones.

### 2. Relevant Learning Context
* Strong academic standing; highly organized; committed to mastering course syllabus.
* Acts as informal peer leader or departmental student facilitator.
* Willing to invest 30 minutes weekly in administrative setup if it guarantees a high-performing study cohort.

### 3. Goals & Motivations
* **Primary Goal:** Establish a reliable, recurring study circle with clear milestones, accountable peers, and structured weekly sessions.
* **Secondary Goal:** Eliminate the administrative chaos of coordinating times, booking rooms, and chasing RSVPs manually.
* **Motivation:** Learns best through collaborative teaching and peer synthesis, but refuses to waste time on uncommitted free-riders.

### 4. Behaviors & Habits
* Drafts study outlines, problem set targets, and exam countdown schedules.
* Uses Google Calendar, Notion, and WhatsApp, but struggles with the fragmentation between them.
* Reaches out to classmates early in the term (Weeks 1–2) before exam panic sets in.

### 5. Study Preferences
* **Primary Preferred Styles:** *Collaborative Problem-Solving*, *Peer Teaching*, and *Structured Exam Preparation*.
* **Preferred Group Size:** Strictly capped at 4 to 6 members.
* **Preferred Cadence:** Weekly recurring sessions (same day, same time) with explicit 90-minute agendas.
* **Preferred Modality:** In-person reserved campus rooms or designated recurring online rooms.

### 6. Scheduling Constraints
* Highly structured weekly schedule; requires consistent recurring slots rather than ad-hoc weekly renegotiation.
* Needs to publish session dates at least 5 days in advance.

### 7. Frustrations & Unmet Needs
* **Attendance Ghosting:** Booking a campus study room only to have 3 out of 4 members cancel with 10 minutes notice.
* **Coordination Fragmentation:** Spending 45 minutes across Doodle polls, WhatsApp pings, and email threads to schedule one session.
* **Uncurated Groups:** Open chat links that balloon to 40 people, turning a focused study circle into an unmanageable broadcast channel.

### 8. Trust, Privacy & Safety Expectations
* Requires full moderator control: Ability to set capacity limits, review join requests, and remove inactive or disruptive members.
* Transparent group dashboard showing member attendance history and commitment.

### 9. Accessibility Considerations
* Desktop-optimized facilitator controls with clear visual hierarchy, accessible modal dialogs, and visible focus indicators (`NFR-A11Y-02`, `NFR-A11Y-03`).

### 10. Key Product Opportunities
* Streamlined Circle Creation Wizard with capacity caps and study style tags (`FR-016`).
* Member Join Request Moderation interface with introductory notes (`FR-018`).
* Integrated Session Scheduler with RSVP tracking (Attending / Tentative / Declined) (`FR-020`, `FR-021`).

### 11. Traceability & Supporting Evidence References
* **Mapped Research Inquiries:** `RQ-02`, `RQ-03`, `RQ-05`, `RQ-06`, `RQ-08`
* **Hypothesis Pointer:** `HYP-01`, `HYP-EM-H01`, `HYP-EM-H02`, `HYP-EM-H12`
* **Post-Fieldwork Validation Sources:** `INT-05`, `OBS-01`, `SUR-Q08`

### 12. Confidence & Limitations
* **Confidence Level:** High operational necessity (without an effective host role, study circles consistently experience entropy).
* **Limitations:** Must verify how many students are genuinely willing to host vs. simply join existing circles.
